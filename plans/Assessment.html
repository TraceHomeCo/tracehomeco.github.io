Same 53 questions as the Trace assessment. Answer based on what's actually in the home — almost none of this is regulated, so a low score reflects the industry, not the homeowner. Most homes we visit score around 25% on a first assessment — this isn't about doing everything perfectly, it's about knowing where things stand.

Scoring logic, if asked: your score is the percentage of things in your home already at the cleanest verified option — clean water filtration, safe cookware, that kind of thing. A few big ones — water, mattress, bedroom air — matter enough that if any are still missing, the score caps at 70% even if everything else is perfect. Once those are covered, the cap disappears and the score just reflects the percentage of everything else at the cleanest option too.
