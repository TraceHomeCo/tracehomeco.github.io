Host self-assessment

About 20 minutes. This gives you an estimated tier and score range for your home right away, and shows what would move it up a level. Nothing here is a judgment on your home. Trace confirms the final tier and score after reviewing your photos.

How it works

  1. Add your details, then answer each item. "Not yet" is a fine answer; it becomes your gap list.
  2. Where it asks for a brand or model, type it in. That is the main thing we review.
  3. See your estimated tier and score range, accept the agreement, and press Send to Trace. Then email photos of the items listed (a label, a tag, the unit itself) as they are today to hello@tracehome.co. A screenshot of your order history works too. Receipts are not required.
  4. Trace reviews your proof and confirms the tier and score. Nothing is approved from this form alone, and no tier can be bought.
Do not live near the property? Add someone on site who can take the photos, or send the ones you have and we will tell you what is missing.
