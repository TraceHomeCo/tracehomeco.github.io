Host self-assessment

About 20 minutes. This gives you an estimated tier and score range for your home right away, and shows what would move it up a level. Nothing here is a judgment on your home. Trace confirms the final tier and score after reviewing your photos.

How it works

  1. Add your details, then answer each item. "Not yet" is a fine answer; it becomes your gap list.
  2. Where it asks for a fiber, brand or model, type what you know. That is the main thing we review. The bar at the bottom shows what is still missing.
  3. See your estimated score range, accept the agreement, and press Send to Trace. Trace then sends you a short list of the photos it needs (a label, a tag, the unit itself) as they are today. A screenshot of your order history works too. Receipts are not required.
  4. Trace reviews your proof and confirms the result. Nothing is approved from this form alone, and no result can be bought.
