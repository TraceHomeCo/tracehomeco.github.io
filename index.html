About Trace

What’s actually in the spaces where you live.

Trace is a home health platform setting the standard for what’s actually in the spaces where people live, sleep, breathe and travel.

Most “clean” claims are self-reported: a brand’s own list, not independent verification. We check each room and identify exactly what’s worth swapping and why, backed by independent certifications.

Questions

Good to know.

Do you test for mold or toxins?

No. We verify the products and materials in your home against independent certifications. We don’t run lab tests.

Do I have to replace everything?

No. The plan ranks swaps by impact and you decide the pace. Many people start with the bedroom and kitchen.

Does this work for renters?

Yes. Most swaps are products, not renovations.

What areas do you serve?

San Diego and Southern California for now.
